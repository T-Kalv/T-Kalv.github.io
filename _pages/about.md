Kalvin is an **undergraduate Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence student** attending **UoL** in the **School of Computer Science and Informatics**. He serves as the **Avionics Lead** and **AI Research Engineer at LASER** (Liverpool Association for Space Engineering Research), a student-led **research group focused on Space-based Engineering**, supported by the Electronics and Electrical Engineering department at UoL. He developed the *[VEGA Rideshare Experiment](https://github.com/UnityRiseUol/pl26-rideshare-experiment)*, the *[PLOTSv1 Ground Station](https://github.com/UnityRiseUol/pl26-groundstation)* and the *[Postflight Analysis Tracker (PAT)v1 Software](https://github.com/UnityRiseUol/pl26-postflight-analysis)* within the *Electronics/Programming Subsystems Team* for *PL-26 (Propulsion Unit for LASER's Spacecraft and Aerospace Research Lite)*. He also served as the joint *Project Lead* for *UoL LASER'S Unity Rise PL-26 Rocket Team 25/26*. Previously, he worked as a **Software Avionics Engineer** and **Machine Learning Software Engineer** on *[LIFTSv1 (LASER's Integrated Flight Tracking System)](https://github.com/UnityRiseUol/pulsar-avionics)* and the *[PLOTSv1 Ground Station](https://github.com/UnityRiseUol/pl26-groundstation) and [PATv1 Software](https://github.com/UnityRiseUol/pl26-postflight-analysis)* respectively, contributing to the *Unity Rise PULSAR Rocket Team 24/25* and recently *Unity Rise PL-26 Rocket Team 25/26* that secured the 1st Place Championship Award at the UKSEDS National Rocketry Championship and Best Rideshare Payload Award.


## 🔬 Research
Kalvin is an **undegraduate researcher** with a strong focus on machine learning, deep learning, computer vision and robotics. His research interests here include **robot learning, physical AI, active perception and intelligent robotic systems**. He maintains a **[technical blog](https://t-kalv.github.io/year-archive/)** where he publishes **research findings**, **summarises academic papers** and **shares insights and tutorials** on advanced topics related to Artifical Intelligence and Computer Science.

- **Robotic Tool Selection & Active Perception Research:** Currently working on an Undergraduate Honours Research Dissertation investigating **force-torque-based active perception for tool selection in robotic scraping** supervised by Dr Gabriella Pizzuto.
- **[VEGA Aerospace Rideshare Experiment](https://github.com/UnityRiseUol/pl26-rideshare-experiment)**: VEGA (Vegetation Evaluation from Ground to Air) is multispectral computer vision rideshare payload to classify vegetation as healthy or not which is designed for embedded systems. Developed for the UKSEDS NRC and Mach competitions, this initiative involves engineering edge-computed, real-time computer vision pipelines capable of custom NDVI (Normalised Difference Vegetation Index) algorithm generation and topographical contour mapping.
- **Adversarial Machine Learning**: Expanding his expertise into AI security, Kalvin engages in hands-on experimentation with neural network vulnerabilities, successfully developing adversarial attack and defence algorithms.
- **[Foundational Deep Learning (MNIST)](https://github.com/T-Kalv/Simple-MNIST-Digit-Classifier-Neural-Network)**: Kalvin implemented a feed-forward neural network (Multilayer Perceptron) from scratch in Python, utilising only core libraries such as NumPy, Matplotlib, Pandas and tqdm. Designed to recognise and classify numerical handwritten digits from the MNIST dataset, this project served to solidify a deep understanding of core mathematics and algorithms, including forward propagation, backpropagation and activation functions (ReLU, Softmax).

To accompany this work, [Kalvin's GitHub repository](https://github.com/T-Kalv/) contains the full codebases, Jupyter Notebooks and corresponding research papers communicating the theory and results of these projects. Feel free to explore the literature and the accompanying repositories.
